# Vision

Why Text Deck exists, who it is for, and what it is not.

## Why
Step away from screens and distraction, back toward more physical media. Carry it anywhere like a laptop, without the laptop screen's cost, weight, noise and distraction. Only the basic functionality.

## Use cases
- Meeting notes
- Writing
- Development on the go

## Beyond a standard writerDeck
CLI agents (Claude Code) for development, agent management, and MCP setups for things like email. Much of the work done by prompting.

## Stage
Personal project first, product maybe later. Budget and logistics deferred; the goal now is to test viability.

## Screen and distraction (clarified)
- The screen is small, battery-efficient and portable. LCD preferred but not required. Non-standard aspect ratios are fine.
- "Distraction" means the things a full screen invites: windows, app switching, browsers opening. Agents, email and MCP are accepted as productive use; the UI stays single-purpose and minimal.
